# AccFo: Your Ultimate Account Manager

Safeguard your sensitive data with AccFo, the all-in-one solution for secure and hassle-free account information storage and management. With its advanced features and top-notch encryption, AccFo ensures your peace of mind while managing your accounts.

## Key Features:

### Secure Account Storage:
Store your account information with confidence. AccFo encrypts your data and stores it securely in an SQL database, ensuring your details remain confidential.

### Master Password Protection:
Your data's security is paramount. AccFo requires a master password for decryption, adding an extra layer of protection against unauthorized access.

### Effortless Data Addition:
Easily add account information, including usernames and passwords. AccFo's encryption process ensures your details are safe and sound.

### Password Generator:
Generate strong and unique passwords effortlessly. Specify your desired length, and let AccFo create robust passwords using cutting-edge algorithms.

### Data Removal:
Keep your account list up to date. AccFo lets you securely remove outdated entries, ensuring deleted data is irrecoverable.

### Secure Data Viewing:
Access your stored information confidently. With the master password, AccFo decrypts and displays your account details, ensuring a seamless and secure retrieval process.

### Multiple Account Management:
Organize with ease. AccFo empowers you to manage and segregate various accounts based on your needs, maintaining a structured approach to online activities.

### Top-Notch Encryption:
AccFo leverages robust encryption techniques, including AES, to fortify your data's security. Your information remains safe even if the database is compromised.

### Trusted Database:
Built on a reliable SQL backend, AccFo ensures efficient data management. Local storage on your device guarantees accessibility and control over your sensitive information.
